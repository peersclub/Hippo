Definition of done, Aug 31: the sync wave is deployed and provable from outside (/health carries a build SHA); every accuracy number on this site came from a run that would have failed loudly if the model were mocked; the demo link survives a phone; and the PRD launch gates that do not need a partner are scored, not estimated.

Scope decision, Aug 14: two items left the MVP by name, not by silence. Hinglish and Devanagari trade phrasing (6d — the single largest item, over a week inside a five-day lane) moves to the pilot phase: the LLM path still handles non-English orders, the deterministic-layer parity work resumes against real pilot traffic, and the 31% / 5% baselines plus the failing-row export keep it measurable the day it resumes. Arabic native-speaker review (1d, Kartik / MENA) was review-blocked, not build-blocked — the ar catalog stays merged and dormant, and the review lands whenever a Gulf partner is real. With those two out, the remaining work fits the weekdays left with the weekends as slack rather than as a requirement. Anything still open on Aug 31 stays on this page with a name against it — not quietly re-dated.
